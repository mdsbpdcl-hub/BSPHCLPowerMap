About these figures
  • Every sub-station, its voltage and its status (existing, under construction, proposed) is read from the BSPHCL “Comprehensive Bihar Map” (13th Plan edition, revised August 2020). Projects shown as under construction then may have been commissioned since; update the data file to bring them current.
  • The map is partly schematic. Points sit where the map draws them, so positions are approximate (within 1–3 km) and a few close to a district border may fall on the wrong side; these were checked and corrected by name.
  • Lines are traced from the colours of the original map and laid over a matched district boundary layer.
  • Area within 20 km: share of the district's area that lies within 20 km (straight line) of an existing grid sub-station of 132 kV or above. It is a simple guide to how long 33 kV feeders need to be.
  • People per sub-station: Census 2011 population divided by existing grid sub-stations in the district.
  • Overall status is an indicative score out of 100: area within 20 km (up to 45), presence of a 220 kV or higher sub-station in the district (25), and people per sub-station (up to 30). Good 70 and above, Watch 50–69, Weak below 50.
